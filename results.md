# Results

Generated from 1,760 Sephora reviews, 154 lore.world reviews and 350 Fragrantica reviews. Every review was read in full and labeled; those labels drive every number below.

## How well the keyword tags matched the full read

- Sephora, fades or too faint: 93.9% agreement (199 read vs 136 keyword)
- Sephora, cigarette or similar smoke: 99.1% agreement (58 read vs 51 keyword)
- lore.world, fades or too faint: 98.7% agreement (24 read vs 22 keyword)
- The keyword tags mostly missed milder complaints ("longevity is medium", "had to reapply later in the day"), so the full-read percentages run a few points higher.

## Sensitivity: paying vs gifted under different choices

Borderline reviews (a neutral "lasts about 4 hours", say) take judgment, so the headline is recomputed several ways. Paying reviewers mention fading about twice as often in each.

| Version | Paying vs gifted, mentioning fading |
|---|---|
| Full-read labels (used in the article) | 18% vs 10% |
| Keyword tags instead of the full read | 14% vs 7% |
| Leaving out the 121 reviews where the gifted question was blank | 20% vs 10% |
| Leaving out every review the verification script flagged as borderline | 12% vs 6% |
